The Holes

    What is pub golf?

    It's like golf except with alcohol instead of balls.

    How do you play?

    1. Each pub is a "hole" and has a "par". Your scorecard tells you the drink and par for each one.
    2. Par is the number of sips you need to finish the drink.
    3. Each sip you take is a point. You need to get the lowest score. Like golf (duh).
    4. Write your score on your card before you leave each pub.
    5. There will be penalties!!
    6. Can't finish a drink? Take par +3 and move on (we've all been there).
    7. Everyone gets one birdie. Swap any drink for non-alcoholic and score par.
